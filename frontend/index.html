Pair a WhatsApp Device
  1. Open WhatsApp on the phone that will host the assistant.
  2. Go to Settings, then Linked Devices.
  3. Select Link a Device and allow camera access.
  4. Scan the code shown to the right to establish the session.
  5. Keep the phone online. The assistant maintains a persistent link.
